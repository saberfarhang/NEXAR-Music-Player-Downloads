# NEXAR Music Player

**A Modern Offline Music Player by Team NEXAR**

NEXAR Music Player is a modern desktop music player designed to provide a smooth, simple, and enjoyable offline music listening experience.

Built with a focus on performance, usability, and a clean user interface, NEXAR Music Player offers an easy way to enjoy and manage your local music collection.

## ✨ Features

- **Offline Playback** — Listen to your favorite music without an internet connection.
- **Modern Interface** — Enjoy a clean and modern user experience.
- **Music Library** — Manage and access your local music collection.
- **Smooth Performance** — Designed for a responsive and comfortable experience.
- **Background Playback** — Keep music playing in the system tray after closing the application window.
- **Improved Full-Screen Player** — Consistent playback control positions across different tracks.
- **Smart Track Titles** — Long song titles are automatically shortened to maintain a clean layout, with full titles available on hover.
- **Multiple Playback Options** — Available in Installer and Portable editions.
